# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis | Excel

## Project Overview

This project analyzes 149,116 coffee shop transactions across three New York City locations from January to June 2023.

The goal of the analysis was to understand sales performance, identify customer purchasing patterns, compare store performance, and uncover trends that could support better business decisions.

I completed the analysis in Microsoft Excel, using formulas, summary tables, KPIs, and charts to transform raw transaction data into business insights.

---

## Business Questions

The analysis focused on answering the following questions:

- How is revenue distributed across the three store locations?
- How has revenue changed over time?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which days of the week perform best?
- What hours generate the most sales?
- What insights could help improve staffing and sales performance?

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains **149,116 transactions** recorded across three coffee shop locations in New York City between January and June 2023.

The transaction data includes information such as:

- Transaction date and time
- Store location
- Product category and type
- Quantity sold
- Unit price

Additional fields were created during the analysis to support reporting, including:

- Revenue
- Hour
- Weekday
- Month

## Key Findings

### Store Performance

Revenue was distributed relatively evenly across the three locations, with each store contributing approximately **32.9%–33.9% of total revenue**.

This suggests that overall revenue was not heavily dependent on a single location.

### Revenue Growth

Revenue increased significantly over the six-month period and more than doubled between February and June.

This indicates strong sales growth during the period covered by the dataset.

### Product Performance

**Coffee and Tea generated approximately 67% of total revenue**, making them the strongest product categories in the business.

This highlights the importance of these core products to overall sales performance.

### Peak Sales Hours

The strongest sales period occurred between **8:00 AM and 10:00 AM**, accounting for approximately **36.7% of daily revenue**.

This represents an important operational period for staffing, stock availability, and customer service.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Ensure sufficient staffing during the 8–10 AM peak period to manage customer demand.
- Maintain strong stock availability for Coffee and Tea products because they contribute the majority of revenue.
- Investigate the factors contributing to monthly revenue growth and determine whether successful patterns can be maintained.
- Continue monitoring individual store performance to identify opportunities for location-specific improvements.
